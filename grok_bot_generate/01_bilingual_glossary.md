# Plate Tectonic Theory｜板塊構造學說
## CLIL 中英對照字彙表（Bilingual Glossary）

| English | 中文 | Simple definition / 簡短定義（EN） | 簡短定義（中） |
|---|---|---|---|
| plate tectonics | 板塊構造學說 | Theory that Earth's outer shell is divided into moving plates | 地球外層分成許多會移動的板塊 |
| tectonic plate | 板塊／構造板塊 | A rigid piece of lithosphere that moves | 岩石圈上一塊會移動的硬殼 |
| lithosphere | 岩石圈 | Rigid outer layer (crust + uppermost mantle) | 由地殼與上地函頂部組成的堅硬外層 |
| asthenosphere | 軟流圈 | Soft, ductile layer under the lithosphere | 岩石圈下方較軟、可流動的層 |
| continental drift | 大陸漂移 | Early idea that continents move across Earth | 大陸會在地球表面移動的早期說法 |
| seafloor spreading | 海底擴張 | New ocean crust forms at mid-ocean ridges | 中洋脊產生新海洋地殼，海底變寬 |
| mantle convection | 地函對流 | Heat-driven circulating flow in the mantle | 地函因熱而循環流動，推動板塊 |
| plate boundary | 板塊邊界 | Zone where two plates meet | 兩板塊交界的地帶 |
| divergent boundary | 張裂／分離型邊界 | Plates move apart | 板塊彼此分開 |
| convergent boundary | 聚合型邊界 | Plates move toward each other | 板塊彼此靠近碰撞 |
| transform boundary | 轉形／錯動型邊界 | Plates slide past each other | 板塊橫向滑過 |
| subduction | 隱沒作用 | One plate sinks under another | 一板塊插入另一板塊下方 |
| subduction zone | 隱沒帶 | Region of subduction, often with trenches | 發生隱沒的地帶，常伴隨海溝 |
| mid-ocean ridge | 中洋脊 | Undersea mountain chain at divergent zones | 張裂邊界的海底山脈 |
| ocean trench | 海溝 | Deep seafloor valley at subduction zones | 隱沒帶形成的深海凹谷 |
| continental collision | 大陸碰撞 | Two continents collide and build mountains | 兩大陸碰撞並形成山脈 |
| Pangaea | 盤古大陸 | Ancient supercontinent | 古生代末期的超大陸 |
| hotspot | 熱點 | Fixed mantle plume that can form volcanoes | 相對固定的地函熱柱，可形成火山 |
| earthquake | 地震 | Sudden ground shaking from plate motion/faults | 板塊或斷層活動造成的地面震動 |
| volcano | 火山 | Opening where magma reaches the surface | 岩漿到達地表形成的出口 |
| ring of fire | 火環 | Pacific belt of volcanoes and quakes | 環太平洋的火山與地震帶 |

### Useful classroom sentence frames｜課堂句型鷹架
- A divergent boundary is where plates move apart.｜張裂邊界是板塊彼此分開的地方。
- At a convergent boundary, plates collide.｜在聚合邊界，板塊會互相碰撞。
- Subduction can create deep ocean trenches and volcanoes.｜隱沒作用可形成深海溝與火山。
- Taiwan is near a convergent plate boundary.｜臺灣位於聚合型板塊邊界附近。
- What evidence supports continental drift?｜有哪些證據支持大陸漂移？
