# CLIL Lesson Outline｜雙語教案大綱
## Theme: Plate Tectonic Theory｜主題：板塊構造學說
Suggested length: 2–3 class periods（建議 2–3 堂）
Language of instruction: English + Chinese scaffolding（英語授課＋中文鷹架）

### Learning outcomes｜學習目標
**Content（內容）**
1. Explain plate tectonics and why plates move.
2. Compare divergent / convergent / transform boundaries with examples.
3. Connect Taiwan seismicity to a convergent boundary setting.

**Language（語言）**
1. Use key academic terms accurately in short explanations.
2. Describe cause–effect with frames: *because / therefore / as a result*.
3. Ask and answer: *What happens when…? / Where do we find…?*

### 4Cs quick check（CLIL 四面向）
- Content: theory + boundary types + Taiwan link
- Communication: glossary talk, map labeling, exit ticket
- Cognition: classify boundaries; infer landforms from motion
- Culture/Community: Taiwan earthquake awareness; Pacific Ring of Fire

### Lesson flow｜課堂流程建議

#### Period 1 — Engage + Explore（Engage／Explore）
1. Warm-up (5′): Show world quake/volcano map. Ask in English: *Where are most earthquakes?* Accept Chinese answers, rephrase in English.
2. Mini input (10′): Plate tectonics in 5 images (lithosphere/asthenosphere/plates).
3. Vocabulary match (15′): Use the bilingual glossary (pair work).
4. Contintent puzzle (15′): Wegener-style fit of South America–Africa (USGS packet activity).
5. Exit ticket (5′): One English sentence + Chinese gloss.

#### Period 2 — Explain + Elaborate（Explain／Elaborate）
1. Boundary stations (25′): Three posters—Divergent / Convergent / Transform. Students rotate and complete a table.
2. Taiwan focus (15′): Philippine Sea Plate vs Eurasian Plate; why frequent earthquakes near Hualien/eastern Taiwan (CWA FAQ level).
3. Speaking task (10′): In pairs, explain one boundary type using sentence frames.

#### Period 3 — Evaluate + Extension（Evaluate／延伸）
1. Short quiz (15′): matching + 3 short answers (EN with ZH support).
2. Optional lab/demo: tennis-ball plate globe (USGS) or cookie/clay divergent model.
3. Reflection: *One new English term I can teach a classmate is…*

### Assessment｜評量
- Formative: glossary check, map labels, exit tickets
- Summative: boundary classification quiz + 80–100 word English summary (allow bilingual notes)

### Differentiation｜差異化
- Support: bilingual word bank on desk; allow first draft in Chinese then translate key sentences
- Challenge: add hotspot vs plate boundary comparison; read one USGS paragraph and paraphrase
